# St. Gennady Online Church Design Variants

Static English-language website previews for St. Gennady Online Church, a
planned American online church with a donation-supported ministry model.

## Variants

- `warm-digital-parish/` - pastoral digital parish with worship, prayer,
  small groups, care, and chapel support funds.
- `modern-worship-broadcast/` - cinematic livestream-first church with live
  hosts, message archive, prayer room, and broadcast giving.
- `mission-discipleship-platform/` - structured discipleship and mission
  platform with cohorts, groups, outreach, and mission funds.

Open `index.html` to compare all three variants.

## Research Inputs

The concepts adapt common patterns from public U.S. online church and church
giving experiences:

- Life.Church Online: online services, live chat, prayer, community, and next
  steps.
- Churchome: app-like access to pastoral team, community, weekly services, and
  generosity-supported digital ministry.
- Saddleback and Elevation: online groups, message libraries, serving paths,
  and clear next steps.
- Planning Center Giving, Pushpay, and Tithe.ly: recurring giving, fund
  selection, church-branded giving, mobile/app giving, and donor history.

## Donation Scope

The donation forms are static design prototypes. Before accepting real gifts,
connect a compliant giving provider such as Planning Center Giving, Pushpay,
Tithe.ly, Stripe, or another authorized processor, then add privacy, tax,
refund, and nonprofit/legal disclosures appropriate to the church.
