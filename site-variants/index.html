St. Gennady Online Church

Three American online church design directions.

Each concept is an English-language static website preview for a donation-supported online church: a pastoral digital parish, a livestream-first worship broadcast, and a discipleship and mission platform.

Life.Church Online: live service, chat, prayer, next steps. Churchome: app-like community, pastoral access, daily connection. Saddleback and Elevation: groups, messages, serving, response paths. Planning Center, Pushpay, Tithe.ly: recurring gifts and fund choice.

Option 1

Warm Digital Parish

A welcoming online chapel for people seeking worship, prayer, pastoral care, belonging, and a transparent way to support the ministry.

Open Variant

Option 2

Modern Worship Broadcast

A cinematic livestream-first experience centered on Sunday service, worship, prayer hosts, message archive, and broadcast support.

Option 3

Mission & Discipleship

A structured church platform for formation cohorts, online groups, local mission prompts, care circles, and mission giving.
